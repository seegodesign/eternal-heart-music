Bansuri naturally invites slower exhale. Sustained tone requires relaxed support and controlled release, which mirrors many breath regulation protocols.

For listeners, this translates somatically. As phrasing lengthens, people often unconsciously entrain to the musical breath cycle and begin breathing more evenly.

For players, daily long-tone work can be a meditation in itself: present attention, subtle body awareness, and emotional settling through breath-led sound.
