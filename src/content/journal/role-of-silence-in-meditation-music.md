Silence is not absence. In contemplative music, silence is structure. It creates contrast, allows emotional processing, and gives the nervous system time to integrate.

Without enough space, music becomes informationally dense. The body may stay activated. With intentional spacing, each tone can land fully before the next gesture arrives.

In production, this means resisting the urge to fill every measure. Spaciousness is often what makes healing music actually feel healing.
