Field recording is both technical and relational. Mic placement, wind protection, and gain staging matter, but so does waiting for the environment to reveal a moment.

I usually collect long beds first, then detail textures: close foliage, distant surf, shoreline movement. In post-production, minimal processing preserves place and realism.

The final mix should feel embodied. If listeners can sense temperature, air, and depth, the recording is doing what it should.
