Regulation is the ability to return to baseline after activation. Sound can support this by providing rhythmic anchors and continuous harmonic environments that calm startle responses.

When sessions are paced well, attention shifts from cognitive overload to sensory grounding. This often feels like warmth in the chest, slower breathing, or spontaneous emotional release.

Small consistent practices are more effective than occasional intensity. Even 10 to 20 minutes of intentional listening can improve recovery capacity over time.
