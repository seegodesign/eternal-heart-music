Recording in nature changes timing. Wind, birds, waves, and rain are never on a metronome. Instead of forcing strict tempo, I build around movement and breath.

A typical session starts with field texture: waterline, leaves, distant birds. Then I layer drone and flute motifs, leaving wide space so natural transients remain audible.

Kauai is not just a backdrop. It is part of the arrangement. The intention is to preserve that living atmosphere so listeners feel placed inside a sonic landscape, not outside it.
